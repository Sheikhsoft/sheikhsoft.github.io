Our Commitment to Financial Privacy

SheikhSoft designs Smart Ledger: AI Accounting with a privacy-first, zero-knowledge philosophy. Your accounting figures, chart of accounts, vouchers, customer lists, and receipt attachments are 100% owned by you. We never sell your financial data, never run third-party advertising trackers, and never train public AI models on your private financial records.

1. Scope and Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how SheikhSoft ("SheikhSoft", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you install and use the Smart Ledger: AI Accounting mobile application (available on iOS and iPadOS via the Apple App Store), our related web services, and the website located at sheikhsoft.com.

By downloading, accessing, or using Smart Ledger: AI Accounting, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our application or services.

2. Information We Collect

We practice strict data minimization. We only collect information strictly required to operate the double-entry accounting engine, deliver AI-powered receipt scanning, sync your workspaces, and manage your Pro subscription entitlements:

A. Account and Profile Credentials
  • Email Address: Used as your unique identity for authentication, password recovery, and critical security notices.
  • Display Name / Business Name: Provided by you during registration or onboarding to personalize your accounting invoices and vouchers.
  • Unique User Identifier (UUID): An opaque cryptographic identifier generated by our backend authentication service (Supabase Auth).
B. Accounting and Ledger Data (User Content)
  • Workspaces and Companies: Names of legal entities, currency symbols, fiscal year settings, and tax configurations you create.
  • Chart of Accounts & Ledgers: Account names, codes, types (Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, Expenses), and balances.
  • Transactions & Journal Entries: Transaction dates, debit/credit amounts, descriptions, categories, voucher numbers, and vendor/customer records.
  • Receipts & Attachments: Images captured via camera or selected from photo library for Optical Character Recognition (OCR) receipt parsing. Uploaded files are stored in private, access-controlled cloud storage buckets.
C. Subscriptions & Payment Data
  • App Store In-App Purchases: All subscription transactions are processed directly by Apple Media Services via StoreKit. SheikhSoft never sees, handles, or stores your credit card number, bank account details, or Apple ID billing credentials.
  • Entitlement Verification: We process an anonymized transaction identifier through RevenueCat solely to verify active entitlement status for Smart Ledger Pro.
D. Technical & Diagnostic Telemetry
  • Crash and Performance Reports: Anonymized stack traces, crash events, and device performance metrics collected via Google Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose bugs and prevent crashes. These logs do not contain user accounting entries.
  • Device Characteristics: Operating system version, device model, and application build number to ensure compatibility.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for core application features:

  • To execute the double-entry accounting engine, calculate financial ledgers, generate balance sheets, and compute profit/loss statements.
  • To power offline-first data synchronization between your local SQLite database on device and your secure cloud workspace.
  • To perform optical character recognition (OCR) on your uploaded receipt images for automatic date, total, and merchant extraction.
  • To verify your subscription tier (Free vs. Smart Ledger Pro) and activate unlimited workspace and advanced export privileges.
  • To respond directly to technical support and account inquiries initiated by you.

4. Security & Data Encryption Architecture

Security is embedded at every architectural tier of Smart Ledger: AI Accounting:

Encryption in Transit

All API communications between the iOS application and cloud endpoints are encrypted using TLS 1.3 with modern cipher suites.

Encryption at Rest

Database records and file storage buckets are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 hardware encryption.

Row-Level Security (RLS)

PostgreSQL policies ensure that data queries are mathematically isolated to the authenticated user ID. Cross-tenant leakage is strictly blocked.

Secure On-Device Storage

Session tokens and sensitive credentials on iOS are persisted inside the Apple Keychain using hardware-backed Secure Enclave.

5. User Account and Data Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

In strict compliance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and CCPA, SheikhSoft provides full user autonomy over account lifecycle and data retention. You have the permanent right to delete your account and all associated accounting records at any time.

In-App Account Deletion Workflow

You can permanently erase your account directly within the app in seconds:

  1. Open Smart Ledger: AI Accounting on your iOS device.
  2. Tap the Profile / Settings icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section at the bottom of the page.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. A security confirmation modal will appear detailing the consequences. Confirm by tapping Delete My Account Permanently.
What Happens When You Delete Your Account:
  • The app executes the cryptographic server RPC delete_user_account().
  • Your user profile, email identity, and authentication records in auth.users are immediately and permanently erased.
  • Foreign-key cascading triggers immediately purge all owned workspaces, ledgers, chart of accounts, vouchers, journal entries, and transactions from our cloud database.
  • All receipt images, attachments, and OCR cache files stored in cloud buckets are purged.
  • Your local on-device SQLite database, cached documents, and Keychain authentication tokens are completely wiped.
  • You are immediately signed out and returned to the initial Welcome screen.
Web-Based Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to your Apple device or have uninstalled the app, you can submit an online deletion request at our dedicated deletion portal:

Visit Account Deletion Page

Or email us directly from your registered account email at support@sheikhsoft.com with the subject "Delete Account Request". Requests are verified and processed within 48 to 72 hours.
